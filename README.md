# Dooto  
### Your Daily Companion

Dooto is a productivity app and your daily companion for managing tasks, building habits, tracking how long it has been since a bad habit occurred, and journaling your mood daily to stay motivated and mindful.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📝 Todo List
- Create, edit, and delete tasks
- Mark tasks as complete
- Organize your daily responsibilities with ease

### 🔁 Habit Tracker
- Track daily habits and build consistency
- Visualize progress over time
- Stay motivated with simple habit tracking

### ⏱️ Since Timer (Bad Habit Tracker)
- Track the last time a bad habit occurred
- See how long it has been since your last relapse
- Reset the timer whenever the habit happens
- Encourages awareness and self-control

### 😊 Mood Journal
- Log your daily mood quickly
- Add notes and reflections
- Identify emotional patterns over time
